# House Construction Cost in Kumbakonam: What Your Budget Should Include

A square-foot rate gives you a starting figure. The harder question is how much money you need to finish the house and move in.

For a house in Kumbakonam, that means looking beyond the main building. The compound wall, water arrangements, approvals and kitchen cabinets may sit outside the construction package. Each can be a perfectly reasonable separate charge. The problem comes when you discover it after fixing your budget.

Here is a practical way to put the numbers together, using Structora’s Kumbakonam package schedule as an example.

## Start with the area being priced

Ask for a floor-by-floor area statement before multiplying anything.

A 1,200 sq ft plot does not automatically mean a 1,200 sq ft house. The proposed building needs to fit the site and applicable planning requirements. If you are building more than one floor, the quote also needs to account for the area on each floor.

Then ask how the builder measures the chargeable area. Are balconies, staircases, covered parking and terrace structures included? Are any priced separately? Get the method in writing so that every quote you compare uses the same basis.

You can use Structora’s [construction cost calculator](https://structoraindia.com/pricing/#estimator) for an initial estimate. Keep the agreed area statement beside it.

## What the Kumbakonam packages work out to

Structora’s Kumbakonam pricing schedule, checked for this article on 16 October 2026, lists these rates:

- Basic: ₹2,349 per sq ft.
- Standard: ₹2,510 per sq ft.
- Premium: ₹2,929 per sq ft.
- Luxury: ₹3,199 per sq ft.

For an illustrative **1,200 sq ft of chargeable built-up area**, the package calculation is:

- Basic: 1,200 × ₹2,349 = **₹28,18,800**.
- Standard: 1,200 × ₹2,510 = **₹30,12,000**.
- Premium: 1,200 × ₹2,929 = **₹35,14,800**.
- Luxury: 1,200 × ₹3,199 = **₹38,38,800**.

These are calculations from Structora’s package schedule, not a survey of average building costs across Kumbakonam. They are also not complete move-in budgets. The final quotation needs to confirm your drawings, site conditions, specifications, exclusions and applicable taxes.

## Read the allowances, not just the package name

“Standard” and “Premium” only become useful descriptions when you know what they contain.

Look at the individual allowances for tiles, doors, windows, plumbing fixtures, wiring and paint. Ask whether a stated tile rate covers the material alone or also includes laying, adhesive, transport and wastage.

For example, suppose a quote allows ₹55 per sq ft for a particular tile and you choose one priced at ₹80. For an illustrative 1,000 sq ft purchase quantity, the material difference alone is ₹25,000. That calculation does not include any change in taxes, wastage or installation costs.

It is much easier to make this choice before ordering. Ask the builder to record your selection and the resulting price change, even when it seems small.

## Put the excluded work into a second list

Structora’s Kumbakonam schedule lists the following outside the square-foot package:

- Compound wall, main gate and service gate.
- Septic tank and soak pit.
- RCC sump.
- Additional elevation and setback-area development.
- Interior work such as a modular kitchen, false ceiling, TV console and panelling.
- Construction electricity connection.
- Plan approval.
- Construction water supply arrangements, listed as borewell and motor.

Your site may not need every item on that list. A suitable existing water supply, for example, changes the conversation. Ask for the actual requirement and price for your plot.

The kitchen deserves particular attention. A countertop, sink and wall tiles do not necessarily include cabinets, drawers, appliances or the complete modular kitchen you have in mind.

Likewise, ask what “elevation development” means in your quotation. A drawing of the front of the house and the cost of executing decorative cladding or other features are separate things.

## Settle the site assumptions before fixing the total

Foundation work needs a design suited to the site and proposed building. A package allowance should not be treated as a foundation recommendation.

Ask your engineer what site investigation is needed, what the estimate assumes, and how the price would change if the required work differs. The same discussion should cover access for deliveries, clearing the plot, filling or levelling, and the proposed floor level relative to the road.

If you want to add another floor later, raise that during design. Allowing space for a staircase is only one part of planning for an extension.

These questions help turn an attractive starting rate into a quotation that describes your actual house.

## Make one complete budget sheet

For the Standard example above, begin with ₹30,12,000 as the package calculation. Then add separate lines for:

- Site-specific work outside the package.
- Approval and professional charges that are excluded.
- Water, drainage, power and other required connections.
- Boundary work, gates and external paving.
- Interiors, appliances and selected upgrades.
- Applicable taxes not already included.
- A contingency amount agreed around the unresolved risks.

Keep land purchase and related purchase costs separate if you are still buying the plot. Include rent during construction and moving expenses in your household budget as well.

There is no honest single add-on figure for all of this without knowing the project. A compact house with simple finishes and existing services needs a different allowance from one with extensive external work and custom interiors.

## Before you accept the quotation

Ask for three documents together: the drawings used for pricing, the specification and exclusion schedule, and the payment schedule.

They should describe the same scope. If the budget is tight, ask which choices can be simplified and what saving each change produces. An engineer should decide any changes affecting structural design.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the construction rate include land?

The package calculation in this article covers construction at the stated rate. Land purchase and associated purchase expenses are separate.

### How much would a 1,200 sq ft house cost under the Standard package?

At the schedule rate of ₹2,510 per sq ft, the package calculation is ₹30,12,000. Confirm the chargeable area, site requirements, exclusions and taxes in a written quotation before treating it as your project budget.

### Are approval charges and interiors included?

The Kumbakonam schedule reviewed for this article lists plan approval and interior works outside the square-foot package. Your signed quotation should state exactly which services and charges are included.

### Can I use the same rate for an additional floor?

Ask for a project-specific assessment. Building above an existing house involves checking the existing structure, access and proposed work; multiplying an area by a new-build rate is not a substitute for that assessment.
